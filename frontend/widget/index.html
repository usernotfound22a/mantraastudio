A stand-in for a customer's website

This page exists to test the widget the way a real visitor would see it: a normal page, no dashboard, no logged-in session. The launcher in the bottom-right corner is the live widget.

How to test a full round trip

  1. Open the dashboard in another tab and go to the inbox.
  2. Open the widget here and ask something the knowledge base can answer.
  3. Watch the thread appear in the dashboard, and reply from the dashboard side.
  4. Come back here: the reply shows up on the next poll, within a couple of seconds.

Try the assistant

Ask a question that is in the knowledge base to see the AI answer, then ask something it cannot know ("what is the warranty on the 40L pack?") to watch it hand over to a human and record the gap.
