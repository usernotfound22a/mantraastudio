## 1. Research Focus
This log records the research conducted for the proposed SocialHub project. The research focuses on the challenges of customer support across multiple digital communication channels and the concepts behind bringing those conversations together in a unified inbox. It also considers the potential role of AI assistance, real-time communication, and secure integration with third-party platforms.

## 2. Background
Businesses increasingly communicate with customers through social networks and their own websites. When each channel is managed separately, support staff may need to switch between different tools to respond to customers. This can make it harder to follow conversation history, coordinate work, and respond consistently.

An omnichannel support system aims to bring conversations from several channels into one place. A shared inbox can help support teams see and manage customer interactions more consistently. SocialHub is proposed as an AI-assisted inbox that brings together website chat and social messaging channels.

## 3. Problem Statement
Managing customer conversations across separate platforms can lead to fragmented communication and inefficient support workflows. Teams may have difficulty keeping track of messages, maintaining context, and identifying unanswered requests. Repetitive questions can also require staff to spend time on similar responses.

The research problem is therefore how a unified support platform can organize conversations from multiple channels and assist support teams while protecting customer and workspace information.

## 4. Research Objectives
The research objectives are to:
1. Understand the challenges of managing customer conversations across multiple channels.
2. Examine the purpose and key characteristics of a unified inbox.
3. Investigate how AI assistance could support customer-service workflows.
4. Identify considerations for real-time communication and third-party messaging integrations.
5. Consider security and privacy requirements relevant to a customer-support platform.

## 5. Research Questions
1. What difficulties do support teams face when customer conversations are spread across different channels?
2. How can a unified inbox improve the organization and handling of those conversations?
3. In what ways could AI assist support staff without removing the need for human oversight?
4. What technical and security considerations arise when connecting social messaging services to one platform?

## 6. Research Method
This initial research log uses a descriptive review of the project brief and the available project documentation. The review considered the stated system purpose, supported communication channels, proposed architecture, and documented Meta integration requirements. The findings below are an initial understanding of the topic and design considerations; they are not presented as results from user interviews, experiments, or a systematic academic literature review.

## 7. Research Findings

### 7.1 Omnichannel communication
The materials reviewed describe a system that brings website chat and social messaging into a shared support experience. The main research insight is that channel integration should preserve a consistent conversation format, so support staff can work with messages without needing to understand each channel's individual data structure.

### 7.2 Unified inbox and conversation management
A centralized inbox is intended to make conversations easier to locate and manage. Conversation history and shared visibility can help staff retain context and coordinate responses. The system should also make it clear which channel a conversation came from and what action is needed next.

### 7.3 AI-assisted support
AI may help with repetitive support work, for example by suggesting responses or helping staff use FAQ information. Such assistance should support—not replace—human decision-making, particularly when a response is sensitive, uncertain, or could affect a customer. The reviewed materials identify AI assistance as part of the project direction; its effectiveness would need to be evaluated during later project work.

### 7.4 Real-time communication
Support workflows benefit from timely updates when new messages arrive or when conversation activity changes. Real-time communication is therefore a relevant consideration for a shared inbox, especially when several team members are working in the same workspace.

### 7.5 Integration, privacy, and security
Connecting to third-party messaging platforms involves more than displaying messages. The reviewed Meta setup material identifies OAuth configuration, permissions, webhook verification, and compliance URLs as integration considerations. Authentication, workspace access boundaries, request validation, and careful handling of access tokens are also important because the platform processes customer conversations.

## 8. Scope of This Research
This log is limited to the research and problem understanding for SocialHub. It documents the project context, research questions, initial findings, and relevant considerations. It does not claim that implementation, user testing, performance measurement, or a formal evaluation has been completed.

## 9. Conclusion
The initial research indicates that managing customer conversations across separate channels can create fragmented support workflows. A unified inbox is a relevant approach for organizing those conversations, while AI assistance and real-time updates may further support staff. Any solution must also consider reliable channel integration, privacy, and security. These findings provide the research context for the project; the system's effectiveness and design choices should be assessed through subsequent development and evaluation.
